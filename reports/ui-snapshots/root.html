About Us

Semiconductor Excellence Since 1971

MagnaChip Semiconductor is a leading designer and manufacturer of analog and mixed-signal semiconductor products. Headquartered in Seoul, South Korea, with global design centers and manufacturing facilities across Asia.

Our power semiconductor solutions are critical enablers in the transition to energy-efficient technologies — from electric vehicles and renewable energy systems to 5G infrastructure and smart consumer devices.
